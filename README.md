## Title
> Dyvert

## Team Members
>Alice Beck, Joe Schmoe, Bob McBobbyface

## Description 
> Dyvert is a simple, comprehensive, easy to use web app with the goal of showcasing various media content from our creators to new consumers who share common interests.
> 
> Our users will have 24/7 access to a social platform that lets them find and save media items (movies, shows, books) that they may want to look into based on a preview shown on a content card.
>
> Content creators (provider) will create cards for movies, shows, and books.
> 
> Users (customer) will scroll through a deck of cards based on their content preferences. They will be able to swipe (DyVert) on the cards; right if they are interested in the media items and want to save them, or left if they are not interested.
> Once they DyVert and add a card to their bucket, they will be able to add reviews for each card they added.
> 
> SysAdmins can monitor posts, they will be able to flag and delete content cards, as well as reviews and comments.
